# Stacks B — Science, Social Sciences, and Fiction

## Location

Stacks B is located on Level 1 of the OPAC FAST NUCES library, to the left of the main entrance. It houses natural sciences, social sciences, management, and fiction.

## Call Number Ranges

| Call Number Range | Subject |
|------------------|---------|
| P–PZ | Language and Literature |
| Q–QH | General Science, Biology |
| QC | Physics |
| HD | Management, Business |
| HF | Commerce, Accounting, Marketing |
| FIC | Fiction (alphabetical by author surname) |

## Fiction Section

The Fiction Section (FIC) is located at the far end of Stacks B. Books are shelved alphabetically by the author's last name. Fiction may be borrowed for 21 days with up to 2 renewals.

## Access Rules

All items in Stacks B (except REF-marked items) are available for checkout. Reference items are marked with red stickers and must remain in the library.
