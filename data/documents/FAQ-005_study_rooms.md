# Group Study Rooms FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## How many group study rooms are available?

The library has **6 group study rooms** on Level 3. Rooms 301–303 accommodate up to 6 people each. Rooms 304–306 accommodate up to 10 people each and include a projector and whiteboard.

## How do I book a study room?

Study rooms can be booked at the circulation desk or via the OPAC online portal. You will need your patron card number. Bookings can be made up to **3 days in advance**.

## What is the maximum booking duration?

Each group may book a room for up to **3 hours per day**. If the room is not occupied after the first 15 minutes of the booking window, the room is released to walk-in patrons.

## Do study rooms have power outlets and Wi-Fi?

Yes. All study rooms have multiple power outlets and are covered by the high-speed library Wi-Fi network (SSID: NUCES-Library). Login with your student or staff credentials.

## Can I use a study room alone?

Study rooms are intended for groups of 2 or more. Individual study is best done in the Reading Room (Level 2) or the individual carrels in Stacks A.
