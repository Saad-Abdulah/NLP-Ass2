Pride and Prejudice, first published in 1813, is Jane Austen's most celebrated novel. It follows the romantic entanglement between the witty Elizabeth Bennet and the proud Mr. Darcy against the backdrop of English country life.

The novel explores themes of class, marriage, morality, and education. It remains one of the most widely read books in the English language and is a staple of literary studies.
