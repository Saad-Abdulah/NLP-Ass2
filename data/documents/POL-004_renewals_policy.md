# OPAC FAST NUCES — Renewals Policy

## How to Renew

Items may be renewed through any of the following channels:
1. **In person** at the circulation desk with your patron card.
2. **OPAC chat** — cite your patron card number and the title or item ID.
3. **Phone** — call the library during operating hours.
4. **Online** — through the OPAC self-service portal.

## Renewal Limit

Each item may be renewed a maximum of **2 times**. After two renewals, the item must be physically returned before it can be borrowed again.

## Eligibility

A renewal is only granted if:
- The item is not overdue at the time of the renewal request.
- No other patron has placed a hold on the item.
- The patron's card account is in good standing (no suspended borrowing privileges).

## New Due Date

A successful renewal extends the loan period by the full standard loan period calculated from the date of renewal, not from the original due date.

For example: a book due on 2026-10-20 renewed on 2026-10-18 will have a new due date of 2026-11-08 (21 days from renewal).

## Failed Renewal

If a renewal is denied, the system will state the reason (hold exists, overdue, renewal limit reached). The patron must return the item by the original due date to avoid further fines.
