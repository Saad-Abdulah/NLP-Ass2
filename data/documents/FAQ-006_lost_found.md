# Lost Property FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## I left something in the library. What should I do?

Go to the circulation desk and describe the item. The library maintains a Lost and Found box at the desk. Items are kept for **30 days** before being donated or disposed of.

## I lost my patron card. What do I do?

Report it immediately to the circulation desk to block the card and prevent unauthorised use. A replacement card costs **PKR 100** and is issued on the same day. All existing loans remain on your account.

## I lost a library book. What is the procedure?

Report the loss to the circulation desk. You will be charged the replacement cost plus a PKR 200 processing fee and any outstanding fines. See the Lost Items Policy (POL-008) for full details.

## Who do I contact for library enquiries outside opening hours?

The OPAC Virtual Desk is available 24/7 for catalogue and policy queries. For urgent account matters outside hours, email the library at library@nu.edu.pk and expect a response the next business day.
