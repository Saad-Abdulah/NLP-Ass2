# Printing and Photocopying FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## Is printing available in the library?

Yes. The library has a **Print and Copy Centre** on Level 1. Printing and photocopying are available Monday–Saturday during library hours.

## What are the printing charges?

| Service | Black & White | Colour |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| A4 single-sided | PKR 5 | PKR 20 |
| A4 double-sided | PKR 8 | PKR 35 |
| A3 single-sided | PKR 10 | PKR 40 |
| Binding (soft) | PKR 50 | — |
| Binding (hard) | PKR 200 | — |

## How do I print from my laptop?

Connect to the library printing network (SSID: NUCES-Library-Print) and use the IP address 10.10.1.20 as the print server. Drivers are available at the circulation desk.

## Can I photocopy library books?

You may photocopy up to **10%** of any library book for personal study. Photocopying entire books or chapters is a copyright violation. The photocopier accepts PKR 5 coins or library print cards.
