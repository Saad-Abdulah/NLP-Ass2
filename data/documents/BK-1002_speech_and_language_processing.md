Speech and Language Processing by Jurafsky and Martin is the leading textbook on natural language processing and computational linguistics. It covers the full pipeline from basic language models to advanced neural NLP methods.

Topics include regular expressions, finite-state automata, n-gram language models, part-of-speech tagging, parsing, syntax, semantics, discourse analysis, information extraction, machine translation, question answering, and dialogue systems.

The third edition (available freely online) integrates modern deep learning approaches. This library copy is the second edition, located in Stacks B under call number P98. It is the primary text for CS-601 (Natural Language Processing) at FAST NUCES.
