# Circulation Supervisor — Ms. Fatima Bukhari

**Position:** Circulation and Access Services Supervisor  
**Office:** Level 1, Circulation Desk  
**Email:** f.bukhari@library.nu.edu.pk  
**Desk Hours:** Monday–Saturday, 08:00–18:00

Ms. Fatima Bukhari oversees all day-to-day circulation operations including checkouts, returns, renewals, holds processing, and patron account management. She has a Master's in Library Science from Karachi University and 8 years of experience in academic libraries.

For issues with:
- Book checkouts and returns
- Patron card registration and replacements
- Overdue notices and fine payments
- Hold pickup and cancellation
- Study room bookings

Contact Ms. Bukhari at the Circulation Desk on Level 1 or via email.
