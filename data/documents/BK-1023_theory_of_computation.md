Sipser's Introduction to the Theory of Computation is the clearest treatment of automata theory, computability, and complexity at undergraduate level. Topics include finite automata, regular languages, context-free languages, Turing machines, decidability, reducibility, and complexity classes (P, NP, NP-completeness).

Required for CS-403 (Theory of Computation) at FAST NUCES. Located in Stacks A, QA267.7. Available. Loan: 21 days, 2 renewals.
