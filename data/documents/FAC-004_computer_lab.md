# Library Computer Lab

## Location

Level 2, north wing (next to the Reference Desk). The Computer Lab has 30 workstations.

## Access

Open to all registered FAST NUCES library patrons with a valid patron card. Log in with your NUCES network username and password.

## Software Available

All workstations have:
- Microsoft Office 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Access).
- Adobe Acrobat Reader.
- SPSS and R for statistical analysis.
- Python (Anaconda distribution with Jupyter Notebook).
- MATLAB.
- Programming IDEs: VS Code, Eclipse, IntelliJ IDEA.
- Screen reader software (JAWS, NVDA) on 4 accessible workstations.

## Internet Access

Workstations have unrestricted internet access through the NUCES campus network. All licensed library databases (IEEE Xplore, ACM DL, Springer, JSTOR, ProQuest) are accessible without VPN from library workstations.

## Printing

Direct printing from workstations to the Print and Copy Centre (Level 1) costs PKR 5/page (B&W) or PKR 20/page (colour). Add funds to your print card at the circulation desk.

## Booking

Workstations are first-come, first-served. During high-demand periods (exam weeks), sessions are limited to 60 minutes per patron if others are waiting.
