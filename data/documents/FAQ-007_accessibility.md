# Accessibility FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## Is the library wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The library entrance on Level 1 is fully accessible with ramp access. All floors are accessible via the lift located near the main entrance. Accessible restrooms are available on Level 1 and Level 2.

## Are there accessible study spaces?

Yes. Dedicated accessible study tables with adjustable heights are available in the Reading Room (Level 2) and near the main entrance on Level 1.

## I have a visual impairment. What assistance is available?

The library provides:
- A screen reader station (JAWS and NVDA installed) in the Computer Lab.
- Magnification software on selected workstations.
- Large-print copies of common policy documents available at the desk.
- Library staff are available to assist with locating and retrieving items.

Contact the circulation desk to arrange specialised assistance in advance.

## I have a hearing impairment. Can I get notification via SMS?

Yes. All hold notifications and overdue reminders can be sent by SMS. Ensure your mobile number is registered with your patron account by contacting the circulation desk.
