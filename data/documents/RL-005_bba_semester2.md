# BBA Semester 2 — Recommended Reading List (FAST NUCES)

## BBA-101: Financial Accounting

- **Financial Accounting** by Weil, Schipper, Francis (BK-1037) — REQUIRED.

## BBA-201: Marketing

- **Principles of Marketing** by Kotler, Armstrong (BK-1038) — REQUIRED. Chapters 1–8.

## BBA-202: Introduction to Computing

- **Python Crash Course** by Matthes (BK-1019) — REQUIRED for lab sessions.
- **Management Information Systems** by Laudon, Laudon (BK-1036) — Supplementary.

Note: Marketing text (BK-1038) has high demand. Borrow early in the semester.
