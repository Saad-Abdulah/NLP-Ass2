# Catalogue Search FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## How do I search the catalogue?

Simply ask the OPAC Virtual Desk by typing the title, author, or a topic keyword. For example:
- "Do you have Introduction to Algorithms?"
- "What books do you have on machine learning?"
- "Is Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces available?"

## What does the availability status mean?

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Available | On the shelf — you can check it out today. |
| Checked out | Borrowed by another patron — you can place a hold. |
| On hold | Being held for another patron — join the hold queue. |
| Reference only | In-library use only — cannot be borrowed. |

## Can I search by subject area?

Yes. Ask about a general topic and the OPAC Virtual Desk will suggest relevant titles from the collection.

## What if the book I want is not in the catalogue?

If the title is not in the FAST NUCES collection, you can:
1. Submit an ILL (Inter-Library Loan) request.
2. Submit a book purchase recommendation at the circulation desk.
