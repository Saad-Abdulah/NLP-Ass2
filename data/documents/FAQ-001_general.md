# General FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## How do I find a book in the library?

Use the OPAC Virtual Desk (this chat system) to search for titles, authors, or topics. The system will tell you the shelf location (call number), availability status, and due date if checked out. For physical searches, shelf guides are posted at the end of each row in Stacks A and B.

## Can I reserve a book that is checked out?

Yes. Place a hold through the OPAC system or at the circulation desk. You will be notified by email and SMS when the item is available for pickup. You have 5 business days to collect it.

## What do I do if the book I need is not in the library?

You have two options:
1. Request it through Inter-Library Loan (ILL). Submit a form at the circulation desk. Processing takes 5–10 business days and costs PKR 150.
2. Suggest a purchase — the library accepts purchase recommendations from students and faculty via a form at the circulation desk.

## How many books can I borrow at once?

Undergraduates and postgraduate students may borrow up to **10 items** simultaneously. Faculty may borrow up to **15 items**.

## Can I renew my books online?

Yes. Use the OPAC Virtual Desk by citing your patron card number, or visit the circulation desk in person.
