# OPAC FAST NUCES — Patron Card Registration

## Who Can Register

All currently enrolled FAST NUCES students, faculty, and administrative staff are eligible for a library patron card at no charge.

## Registration Requirements

To register, visit the circulation desk with:
1. Valid FAST NUCES student or employee ID.
2. Active university email address (for notifications).
3. Current contact mobile number.

Registration is processed immediately. The patron card is issued on the spot.

## Card Usage

The patron card (physical or digital) is required for:
- Checking out books and other items.
- Renewing items in person or by phone.
- Placing holds.
- Accessing restricted reference areas.

## Lost or Stolen Cards

Report a lost or stolen card immediately to the circulation desk. A replacement card will be issued for a fee of PKR 100. Outstanding loans remain the responsibility of the cardholder until the card is reported lost.

## Card Expiry

Student cards expire at the end of each academic year and must be renewed with valid enrolment proof. Faculty cards are renewed annually with a valid contract. Cards not renewed within 30 days of expiry are deactivated.
