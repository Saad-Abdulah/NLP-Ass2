# Reference Librarian — Mr. Usman Ghani

**Position:** Reference and Instruction Librarian  
**Office:** Level 2, Reference Desk  
**Email:** u.ghani@library.nu.edu.pk  
**Desk Hours:** Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00

Mr. Usman Ghani is the primary point of contact for research assistance at FAST NUCES. He holds an MPhil in Information Management from the University of Punjab and a Bachelor's in Computer Science from FAST NUCES.

Mr. Ghani specialises in helping students and faculty with:
- Advanced catalogue and database searches.
- Citation management (Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote).
- Literature review strategies for research papers and theses.
- IEEE Xplore and ACM Digital Library access and search techniques.
- Research methodology consultations for FYP and graduate students.

One-on-one research appointments can be scheduled via email or at the Reference Desk.
