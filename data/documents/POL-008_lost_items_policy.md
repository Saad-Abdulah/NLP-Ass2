# OPAC FAST NUCES — Lost and Damaged Items Policy

## Lost Items

An item is declared lost when it has not been returned **60 days** after the due date. When this occurs:

1. The patron's account is charged the replacement cost of the item.
2. A processing fee of **PKR 200** is added.
3. All overdue fines up to the maximum cap (PKR 200 per item) are applied.
4. The patron's borrowing privileges are suspended until full payment is made.

The replacement cost is assessed at the current market price of the item or **PKR 2,000 minimum**, whichever is greater. For rare or out-of-print items, the Head Librarian determines the replacement cost.

## Found Items

If a patron finds and returns a declared-lost item, any fine up to the maximum cap remains payable, but the replacement charge is waived. The processing fee (PKR 200) is not waived.

## Damaged Items

Items returned with significant damage (torn pages, water damage, missing covers, broken spines) will be assessed by library staff. If the item is still usable, a repair fee of PKR 300–PKR 1,000 is charged. If the item is no longer usable, it is treated as a lost item and the full replacement cost applies.
