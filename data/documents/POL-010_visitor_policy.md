# OPAC FAST NUCES — Visitor and Guest Access Policy

## General Visitors

Members of the public and students from other institutions may access the OPAC FAST NUCES library as day visitors during regular operating hours. Visitors must:
1. Register at the circulation desk with valid photo ID.
2. Obtain a Visitor Day Pass (valid for one day).
3. Leave bags at the bag storage area near the entrance.

Visitors may use library resources within the library premises (reading, reference materials, in-library computers) but may **not** borrow items or access restricted areas.

## Alumni Access

FAST NUCES alumni with a valid alumni card may access the library and borrow up to **3 items** at a time. Alumni must renew their alumni card with the Registrar's office annually. Overdue fines apply at standard rates.

## External Researcher Access

Researchers from other institutions requiring access to FAST NUCES thesis archives or special collections must apply in writing to the Head Librarian at least **5 business days** in advance. Approval is granted at the Head Librarian's discretion.
