# OPAC FAST NUCES — Overdue Fines Policy

## Fine Rates

Overdue fines are charged at **PKR 20 per item per day** for all loan types. Fines begin accruing from midnight on the day after the due date.

## Maximum Fine Per Item

The maximum fine per item is capped at **PKR 200 per item**. Once a fine reaches this cap, it does not increase further, but the item must still be returned.

## Lost Items

If an item is not returned within **60 days** of the due date, it is declared lost. The patron is charged:
- The replacement cost of the item (assessed at current market price or PKR 2,000, whichever is greater).
- A processing fee of PKR 200.
- All accrued overdue fines up to the cap.

## Fine Payment

Fines may be paid at the circulation desk (cash or card). Patrons with unpaid fines above PKR 100 will have their borrowing privileges suspended until the balance is cleared.

## Appeals

Fine appeals must be submitted in writing to the Head Librarian within 14 days of the fine being issued. Fines will not be waived for overdue items except in documented medical or emergency circumstances.
