# Technical Services Librarian — Mr. Hassan Raza

**Position:** Acquisitions and Cataloguing Librarian  
**Office:** Level 1, Back Office (L-105)  
**Email:** h.raza@library.nu.edu.pk

Mr. Hassan Raza manages the acquisition of new materials (books, journals, databases) and maintains the OPAC catalogue. He holds a Bachelor's in Library Science and a Master's in Information Technology from COMSATS.

Mr. Raza processes:
- Book purchase recommendations from students and faculty.
- New acquisitions and catalogue record creation.
- Inter-Library Loan requests (processing and coordination).
- Withdrawal of outdated or damaged items.

To submit a book purchase recommendation, email Mr. Raza with the title, author, ISBN, publisher, and a brief justification for the acquisition.
