# OPAC FAST NUCES — Holds and Reservations Policy

## Placing a Hold

Patrons may place a hold on any item that is currently checked out. Holds cannot be placed on items that are currently available on the shelf — patrons should proceed directly to checkout.

Holds can be placed at the circulation desk or through the OPAC online system. There is no charge for placing a hold.

## Hold Queue

Holds are filled in the order they are placed. If multiple patrons have placed holds on the same item, the earliest-placed hold is filled first.

## Pickup Notification

When a held item is returned and processed, the next patron in the queue will be notified by email and SMS within one business day.

## Pickup Window

Once notified, the patron has **5 business days** to pick up the held item. If the item is not collected within this window, the hold is cancelled and the next patron in the queue is notified.

## Maximum Holds

A patron may have up to **5 active holds** at any time. A hold on an item that is already on hold for 5+ other patrons may result in a wait of several weeks.
