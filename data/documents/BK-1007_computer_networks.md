Computer Networks: A Top-Down Approach by Kurose and Ross presents the modern networking curriculum starting from the application layer and working down to the physical layer. The book uses the internet as its primary example throughout.

Chapters cover application-layer protocols (HTTP, FTP, DNS, SMTP), transport layer (TCP, UDP, congestion control), network layer (IP, routing algorithms, BGP, OSPF), link layer (Ethernet, ARP, MAC), and wireless networks.
