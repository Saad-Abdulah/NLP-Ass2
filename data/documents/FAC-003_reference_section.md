# Reference Section and Reading Room

## Location

The Reference Section is on Level 2 of the OPAC FAST NUCES library, directly accessible by the main staircase or lift. This is a silent study area.

## Contents

The Reference Section contains:
- Encyclopaedias and dictionaries (general and subject-specific).
- Atlases and statistical yearbooks.
- Handbooks and standards (IEEE, ISO, ASTM).
- Bound journal volumes (older issues).
- FAST NUCES thesis and dissertation archive (2010–present).
- High-demand textbooks designated as reference-only (red sticker).

## Rules

- Silent zone — no conversations permitted.
- Mobile phones must be on silent; step outside to take calls.
- No food or drinks.
- Items may NOT be removed from the Reference Section.
- Photocopying is limited to 10% of any single work.

## Seating

The Reading Room adjacent to the Reference Section has 60 seats including accessible desks near the entrance. Natural light from the south-facing windows makes this the most popular study area.
