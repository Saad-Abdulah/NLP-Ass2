# Individual Quiet Study Zone — Level 2 South Wing

## Overview

The Individual Quiet Study Zone on Level 2 (south wing) is the library's primary silent study area, separate from the main Reading Room. It offers 40 individual study pods with privacy screens.

## Features

- 40 individual pods with built-in power outlets (2 sockets per pod).
- USB-A and USB-C charging ports at each pod.
- Adjustable task lighting.
- Ergonomic chairs.
- High-speed Wi-Fi (NUCES-Library network).
- Sound-dampening panels between pods.

## Rules

- Absolute silence required — no talking, no phone calls, no music through speakers.
- Earphones/headphones are permitted.
- No food; sealed water bottles only.
- Pods cannot be reserved — first-come, first-served.
- During peak study periods (exam weeks), pods are limited to 3 hours per patron if the area is at capacity.

## Access Hours

Accessible during all regular library hours (see POL-009 for hours schedule). The Quiet Study Zone is typically the last area closed before library shutdown.
