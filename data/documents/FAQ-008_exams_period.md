# Exam Period FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## Does the library extend hours during exams?

Yes. During mid-term and final exam periods, the library extends its weekday hours to **08:00 – 24:00** and Saturday hours to **09:00 – 22:00**. Sunday hours (normally closed) become **12:00 – 20:00** during exam week. Extended hours are announced two weeks in advance on the library notice board and OPAC portal.

## Can I borrow more books during exams?

The standard borrowing limit (10 items for students) applies year-round. However, loan periods for items returned during exam week are not extended — due dates remain as issued.

## What is the recall policy during exams?

If you have items that others urgently need (evidenced by a hold), the library may issue a recall notice requiring return within 7 days even if the standard loan period has not elapsed. Please check your email regularly during exam periods.

## Are study rooms available 24/7 during finals?

Study rooms are available until 23:30 during finals week (last booking at 21:00 for a 2-hour slot). Overnight access to the library is not available — the building closes at 24:00.
