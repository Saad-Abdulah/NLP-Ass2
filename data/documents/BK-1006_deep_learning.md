Deep Learning by Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville is the comprehensive textbook on modern machine learning with neural networks. It covers linear algebra, probability, information theory, numerical computation, machine learning basics, feedforward networks, regularisation, optimisation, convolutional networks, recurrent networks, and generative models.
