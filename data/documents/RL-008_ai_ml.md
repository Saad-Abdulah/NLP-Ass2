# AI and Machine Learning Specialisation — Reading List (FAST NUCES)

## Core Texts

- **Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach** by Russell, Norvig (BK-1009) — Foundation.
- **Deep Learning** by Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville (BK-1006) — Advanced theory (reference only).
- **Speech and Language Processing** by Jurafsky, Martin (BK-1002) — NLP component.

## Practical Tools

- **Python Data Science Handbook** by VanderPlas (BK-1020) — Hands-on Python/ML.
- **Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning** by Bishop (BK-1018) — Graduate reference (reference only).

## Data Mining

- **Data Mining: Concepts and Techniques** by Han, Kamber, Pei (BK-1031) — Applied mining.

Students are encouraged to borrow core texts early — demand is high, especially at midterm and project season.
