# CS Semester 3 — Recommended Reading List (FAST NUCES)

## CS-201: Object-Oriented Programming

- **Clean Code** by Robert C. Martin (BK-1004) — REQUIRED. Read chapters 1–7 before Week 4.
- **Design Patterns** by Gamma, Helm, Johnson, Vlissides (BK-1011) — Supplementary. Chapters on Creational and Structural patterns.
- **The Pragmatic Programmer** by Thomas, Hunt (BK-1021) — Recommended.

## CS-212: Computer Organisation and Architecture

- **Computer Organization and Design** by Patterson, Hennessy (BK-1015) — REQUIRED. Chapters 1–4.
- **Digital Design** by Mano, Ciletti (BK-1028) — Supplementary for digital logic review.

## MATH-201: Probability and Statistics

- **Probability and Statistics for Engineering and the Sciences** by Devore (BK-1012) — REQUIRED. Chapters 1–5.
- **Linear Algebra and Its Applications** by Strang (BK-1013) — Supplementary.

All required texts are available in the library. Please check catalogue status before visiting.
