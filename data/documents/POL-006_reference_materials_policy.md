# OPAC FAST NUCES — Reference Materials Policy

## Definition

Reference materials are items designated for in-library use only. These include but are not limited to:
- Encyclopaedias and dictionaries.
- Atlases and maps.
- Handbooks and manuals marked REF or REFERENCE.
- Periodicals, journals, and magazines.
- Newspapers.
- Theses and dissertations from the NUCES archive.
- Items marked with a red reference sticker.

## In-Library Use

Reference items may be used freely within the library during operating hours. They cannot be checked out, renewed, or placed on hold.

## Photocopying

Patrons may photocopy up to 10% of a reference work for personal study purposes, subject to copyright restrictions. The library photocopier is available near the reference section (PKR 5 per page).

## Requesting Special Access

In exceptional circumstances (e.g. for an extended research project), faculty may request temporary supervised overnight lending of specific reference items. Such requests require written approval from the Head Librarian and are granted on a case-by-case basis.
