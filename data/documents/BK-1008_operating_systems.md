Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces (OSTEP) is a freely available and highly regarded OS textbook. The three pieces are: virtualisation (CPU and memory), concurrency (threads and locks), and persistence (file systems and I/O).

The book is known for its clear writing style and is used in OS courses worldwide. At FAST NUCES, it supplements the main OS text for CS-321 (Operating Systems).
