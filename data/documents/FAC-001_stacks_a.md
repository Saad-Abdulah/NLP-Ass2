# Stacks A — Engineering and Computer Science Collection

## Location

Stacks A is located on Level 1 of the OPAC FAST NUCES library, to the right of the main entrance. It houses the engineering, computer science, and mathematics collections.

## Call Number Ranges

| Call Number Range | Subject |
|------------------|---------|
| QA1–QA99 | Mathematics |
| QA76–QA76.9 | Computer Science, Programming |
| QA267–QA268 | Theoretical Computer Science |
| TK1–TK9999 | Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Telecommunications |

## Access Rules

All items in Stacks A (except those marked REF) are available for checkout with a valid patron card. Items not on the shelf may be checked out — verify status via the OPAC Virtual Desk before visiting.

## Study Carrels

Stacks A contains 12 individual study carrels along the east wall (near QA76 shelves). These are first-come, first-served. Carrels have power outlets and are under the library's Wi-Fi coverage area.

## Reshelving

Items removed from the shelf for in-library reading should be placed on the reshelving trolley (blue trolley) at the end of each row. Do not reshelve items yourself — incorrect shelving makes them difficult to find.
