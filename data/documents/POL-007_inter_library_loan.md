# OPAC FAST NUCES — Inter-Library Loan (ILL) Policy

## Overview

If an item you need is not available in the OPAC FAST NUCES collection, you may request it through the Inter-Library Loan (ILL) service. ILL allows patrons to borrow materials from partner academic libraries.

## Eligibility

ILL is available to all registered FAST NUCES patrons (students and faculty). Undergraduates must have faculty supervisor approval for ILL requests.

## How to Request

Submit an ILL request form at the circulation desk or via the OPAC online portal. Include:
- Full title, author, edition, and ISBN.
- Your patron card number.
- The course or research purpose (required for students).

## Processing Time

ILL requests typically take **5–10 business days** to fulfil, depending on the lending institution's availability. Rush requests (for thesis deadlines) may be submitted with Head Librarian approval.

## Loan Period and Fees

The loan period for ILL items is determined by the lending library, typically 14–21 days. ILL items may not always be renewable. A small processing fee of **PKR 150 per request** applies. Overdue fines for ILL items may be higher — the borrowing library passes on any fines charged by the lending library.
