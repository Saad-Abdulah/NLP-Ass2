# Head Librarian — Dr. Ayesha Tariq

**Position:** Head Librarian, OPAC FAST NUCES  
**Office:** Level 1, Room L-101  
**Email:** a.tariq@library.nu.edu.pk  
**Office Hours:** Monday–Thursday, 10:00–12:00

Dr. Ayesha Tariq has led the FAST NUCES library for over 12 years. She holds a PhD in Library and Information Science from the University of Karachi and a Master's in Computer Science from FAST NUCES. Her research interests include digital library systems, knowledge organisation, and information literacy.

Dr. Tariq oversees all library operations including acquisitions, cataloguing, circulation, reference services, and digital resources. She coordinates the ILL programme with partner institutions and chairs the Library Advisory Committee.

Patrons wishing to appeal fines, request special collections access, or discuss substantial purchase recommendations should contact Dr. Tariq's office directly or via email.
