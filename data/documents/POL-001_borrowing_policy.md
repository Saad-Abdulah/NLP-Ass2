# OPAC FAST NUCES — Borrowing Policy

## Loan Periods

The standard loan period for most books and monographs is **21 days** from the date of checkout. New releases marked with a "NEW" sticker are subject to a shorter loan period of **7 days** and may not be renewed.

Graduate students and faculty may borrow items for up to **28 days** with a valid faculty or graduate ID card. Periodicals, journals, and newspapers cannot be borrowed and are for in-library use only.

## Maximum Active Loans

Each patron may have up to **10 items** checked out simultaneously. Once the limit is reached, no further checkouts are possible until items are returned or renewed.

## Renewal Rules

Items may be renewed up to **2 times** provided:
1. No other patron has placed a hold on the item.
2. The item is not overdue at the time of renewal.
3. The patron has not exhausted their renewal limit for that item.

Renewals can be processed in person at the circulation desk, by phone, or via the OPAC online system. Each renewal extends the loan by the standard loan period from the date of renewal.
