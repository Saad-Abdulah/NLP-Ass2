# Thesis and Dissertation Archive FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## Does the library have FAST NUCES theses and dissertations?

Yes. The FAST NUCES Thesis Archive holds all approved undergraduate final-year projects (FYPs) and graduate theses from 2010 to present. They are located in the Reference Section and are accessible to all registered library patrons.

## Can I borrow a thesis?

No. Theses and dissertations are reference-only materials and cannot be removed from the library. Patrons may read them in the Reference Section and photocopy up to 10% of the document for personal study.

## How do I search for a thesis?

Use the OPAC catalogue system (thesis search is separate from the main catalogue) or ask at the reference desk. Search by author name, supervisor name, topic, or year.

## Can I access NUCES theses digitally?

Selected theses are available digitally through the ProQuest Dissertations & Theses database. The library is gradually digitising older theses — check the portal for the latest additions.

## I am submitting my FYP. What are the submission requirements?

Final-year projects must be submitted to the library in:
1. **Hardbound printed copy** (one copy).
2. **PDF on CD or USB** (labelled with student ID, title, and year).

Submission is required before the Registrar issues the degree clearance. The library submission desk is open Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00.
