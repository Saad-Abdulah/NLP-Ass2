Introduction to Algorithms, commonly known as CLRS after its four authors, is the definitive undergraduate and graduate textbook on algorithm design and analysis. This fourth edition covers a broad range of algorithms in depth, making them accessible to all levels of readers.

The book presents algorithms in a concise pseudocode style that is readable without knowing a specific programming language. Topics include sorting and order statistics, data structures, graph algorithms, dynamic programming, greedy algorithms, amortized analysis, and NP-completeness.
