# Digital Resources FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## What digital databases does the library provide access to?

FAST NUCES library provides access to the following databases (on-campus access and via VPN for off-campus):
- **IEEE Xplore** — Engineering and computer science journals and conference papers.
- **ACM Digital Library** — Computing research publications.
- **Springer Link** — Multidisciplinary e-books and journals.
- **JSTOR** — Humanities, social sciences, and sciences archives.
- **ProQuest** — Theses, dissertations, and business research.

## How do I access databases from off-campus?

Connect via the FAST NUCES VPN using your university credentials, then access the library portal at library.nu.edu.pk. Database access is restricted to enrolled students and faculty.

## Are e-books available?

Yes. Springer Link provides e-book access for many titles available in print at the library. Check the library portal for the full list of available e-books.

## How do I request a digital article not in our databases?

Submit an inter-library loan (ILL) request specifying the journal name, volume, issue, and article title. Digital article delivery is typically faster than physical ILL — often within 2–3 business days.
