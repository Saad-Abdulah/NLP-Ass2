# OPAC FAST NUCES — Library Hours and Quiet Zones

## Operating Hours

| Day | Hours |
|-----|-------|
| Monday – Friday | 08:00 – 22:00 |
| Saturday | 09:00 – 18:00 |
| Sunday | Closed |
| Public holidays | Closed (check notice board for exceptions) |

The OPAC Virtual Desk is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for catalogue queries, policy questions, and account lookups.

## Quiet Zones

The Reference Section and Reading Room (Level 2) are designated silent study areas. Mobile phones must be on silent and all conversations must be taken outside these areas.

The Main Floor (Level 1) is a quiet zone — hushed conversations are permitted but normal speaking volume is not.

Group study rooms on Level 3 are available for collaborative work. They can be booked in advance at the circulation desk for up to 3 hours per session.

## Food and Drink

No food is permitted anywhere in the library. Sealed water bottles are allowed. Hot drinks may be consumed in the library lobby only.
