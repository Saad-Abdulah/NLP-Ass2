# Fines and Account FAQs — OPAC FAST NUCES

## How do I check my fines?

Cite your patron card number to the OPAC Virtual Desk or visit the circulation desk in person. You can also check your account on the OPAC online portal.

## My fine seems incorrect. What should I do?

Submit a written appeal to the Head Librarian within **14 days** of the fine being issued. Include your patron card number, the item ID, and a brief explanation. Fines are only waived in documented medical or emergency circumstances.

## Can I pay my fines online?

Online fine payment is not currently available. Fines must be paid in person at the circulation desk (cash or university payment card accepted).

## My borrowing is suspended. How do I restore it?

Clear all outstanding fines above PKR 100 at the circulation desk. Return all overdue items. Borrowing privileges are restored immediately once the account balance is cleared and all items are returned.

## What happens to my account at graduation?

All items must be returned and all fines paid before the Registrar issues degree clearance. The library will issue a library clearance certificate upon request once the account is settled.
