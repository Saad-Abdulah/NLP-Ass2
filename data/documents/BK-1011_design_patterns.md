Design Patterns, written by the Gang of Four (GoF), is the foundational reference for object-oriented software design. It catalogues 23 classic design patterns divided into creational, structural, and behavioral categories.

Patterns covered include Singleton, Factory Method, Abstract Factory, Builder, Prototype (creational); Adapter, Bridge, Composite, Decorator, Facade, Flyweight, Proxy (structural); Chain of Responsibility, Command, Interpreter, Iterator, Mediator, Memento, Observer, State, Strategy, Template Method, Visitor (behavioral).
