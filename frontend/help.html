OPAC FAST NUCES

Using the virtual reference desk

This chat connects you to the NUCES Virtual Desk: an automated OPAC assistant for catalogue lookup, borrowing policy, loan due dates, and renewal guidance.

What you can ask

  • Whether a title is in the catalogue, shelf location, and availability status
  • Borrowing period, fines, holds, and renewal rules
  • Due dates and renewal options for your patron card

The assistant does not answer general trivia, jokes, coding homework, weather, or other non-library requests.

If you ask for your name without a patron card, the desk will not guess—it is the virtual assistant, not your library account. Use card P-78421 when you want account details for the sample record on file.

Patron card P-78421

For this deployment, the integrated catalogue includes a sample patron record with card number P-78421 (Alex Rivera). Use this card when you want to:

  • See which items are currently on loan and their due dates
  • Ask whether a title on that account can be renewed under library policy

Example: Card P-78421: what is due this week?

In a full library system, you would sign in with your own card; here the sample card represents that account lookup flow using the same policy rules.

Chat controls

  • Send — submit your message (Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line)
  • New session — clear this browser conversation and start fresh server-side history
  • Connected / Offline — WebSocket status; reconnect automatically when possible
  • Suggested questions — expand the dropdown in the sidebar for quick prompts
  • Chats — one empty New chat at a time until you send a message; then you can open another. Use ⋯ on a chat for rename or delete.

While a reply is generated

You will see NUCES Virtual Desk is thinking until the first part of the answer appears. Replies stream in word by word. Wait until the line disappears and the input is enabled before sending the next message.
